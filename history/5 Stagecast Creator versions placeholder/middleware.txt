For exhibition purposes I started using a gamepad-to-keyboard mapper. 
The most recent one was ControllerMate (https://www.orderedbytes.com/controllermate/) for the show at the Institute for Contemporary Art in San Jose, CA. 
They had me ship to them two Mac Mini's loaded with the Stagecast generated game executables and browser plug-in, iCab Browser set to boot into kiosk mode, and ControllerMate as middle-ware.
ContollerMate allowed for mapping of SNES, and even NES style, wired, USB controllers.

Prior to this particular version I had used Carvware's GamePad Companion (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gamepad-companion/id428799479?mt=12) and USB Overdrive (https://www.usboverdrive.com)
